Privacy Policy

SEVENVERSE — an unofficial BTS fan community app
Effective date: 5 November 2026

SEVENVERSE ("we", "our", "the app") is an unofficial fan community for BTS ARMY members. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to HYBE Corporation or BTS. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the app, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: when you register, we collect your email address and a username/display name. Your password is handled securely by Firebase Authentication and is never stored in plain text by us.
  • Profile information: profile photo, bio, and preferences you choose to add.
  • Content you create: posts (text, images, videos), comments, likes, chat messages (text, voice notes, videos), and community interactions.
  • Presence and usage data: basic online/offline status and last-active timestamps so the community can see who is online, plus app version information used for compatibility.
  • Device information: limited technical data needed to operate the app (for example, device type and app version).

2. How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account and let you sign in.
  • To show your posts, messages, and profile to other community members as part of the app's social features.
  • To display online presence to other users.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent abuse, and enforce community rules.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show third-party advertisements.

3. Third-party services

The app uses the following Google services to operate. Their own privacy policies apply:

  • Firebase Authentication — sign-in and account security. (Firebase Privacy)
  • Cloud Firestore — stores your profile, posts, messages, and community data.
  • Cloudinary — stores the photos and videos you upload.
  • Google Fonts — loads the app's fonts; Google may receive your IP address when fonts load. (Google Privacy Policy)

4. Data retention

We keep your account and content for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile and personal data are removed; posts or messages you shared publicly in the community may remain in anonymized form where other users have already interacted with them.

5. Account deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by emailing lastsummerat@gmail.com. An in-app account deletion option is being added to the app.

6. Children's privacy

SEVENVERSE is a fan community that may be used by teenagers. The app is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), please do not create an account. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and Firebase security rules that restrict database access to signed-in users. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to ask us to stop processing it. Contact us at lastsummerat@gmail.com and we will respond to your request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
